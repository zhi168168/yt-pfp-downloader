Accounts and daily allowances

Sign-in is required for channel lookups, previews and downloads. Free accounts receive one unique channel per UTC day. Different links resolving to the same channel and repeated downloads do not cost extra uses that day. Failed lookups do not count. Allowances reset at 00:00 UTC and do not roll over.
