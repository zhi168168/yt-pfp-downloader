What counts as one use?

One unique YouTube channel per account per UTC day. A channel lookup unlocks its profile information, previews and every available avatar size. Reopening that channel, changing sizes or using a video link from the same channel does not use more of your daily allowance.

When does my allowance reset?

Every day at 00:00 UTC. Unused allowance does not roll over. Failed lookups do not count. Opening a channel again on a new UTC day uses one of that day’s slots.

How do monthly plans work?

Starter is $3.88/month for 100 channels per day. Pro is $9.88/month for 1,000 channels per day. No trial or annual commitment. Cancel renewal through My account; access continues through the successfully paid period. A failed renewal or expired period returns your account to Free.

Can I request a refund?

For your first paid subscription, request a full refund within 7 calendar days if your total paid channel uses since that payment are no more than 10 for Starter or 100 for Pro. This is a cumulative threshold, not a daily refund threshold. Support reviews requests. Duplicate charges, qualifying service interruptions and mandatory legal rights are covered in our Refund Policy.

Does paying create a sharper original?

All plans offer the same available image sizes. Larger variants may be scaled by YouTube; a paid plan increases your daily channel allowance.
