About YT PFP

YT PFP Downloader is a browser tool with a free daily allowance and optional monthly plans for viewing and saving the current profile picture of a public YouTube channel.

One link, one clear result

Paste a channel URL, @handle or video link, confirm the channel, then choose an available image size. The service retrieves public channel information through the YouTube Data API. Avatar files come from Google’s image delivery servers.

What “high resolution” means here

The tool checks nine image variants, up to 1280px, and displays their returned dimensions. A larger image may have been scaled by the source and does not guarantee more native detail or the original uploaded file.

This website is independently operated. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by YouTube or Google. Channel names, logos and profile pictures belong to their respective owners.
