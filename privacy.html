4. Cookies and your choices

Necessary HTTP-only session cookies maintain sign-in. Sessions last up to 7 days and may refresh while you use the service. Your selected channel and size may be held in sessionStorage to resume after sign-in; this is limited to the browser session. You can clear browser storage, although clearing essential cookies signs you out.

Google Analytics is off by default. Choose “Allow analytics” to enable it or “Essential only” to decline it. Both choices leave the downloader and account functions available. Open Cookie settings at any time to change your choice. We remember the choice on this browser for 180 days unless you clear or change it. Withdrawal disables future analytics and removes accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies; it cannot recall data already sent. Where storage is blocked, your choice may only persist for the current page.

We use no advertising pixels or targeted-advertising cookies. Google Analytics data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. Google controls its own processing and cookie lifetimes; browser settings also let you block or remove cookies.

7. Retention

  • Account profile and linked sign-ins: while your account exists. Confirmed account deletion removes the active profile and sessions. We aim to complete any remaining deletion review within 30 calendar days, subject to lawful exceptions. Canceling a subscription does not delete your account.
  • Daily channel records: retained while your account exists to administer quota, refunds and abuse prevention; removed on confirmed account deletion. Daily reset changes the current allowance, not historical record retention.
  • Payment, consent and accounting records: up to 7 years after the end of the related transaction/subscription for accounting, disputes and fraud prevention, or longer only when legally necessary. Limited account identifiers and closure markers may remain with those records after account deletion.
  • Support correspondence: up to 2 years after closure of the request, unless needed for an ongoing dispute or legal obligation.
  • Application caches: public profile results are usable from cache for up to 15 minutes and images for up to one hour. Signed image links expire after one day. Expired cache entries are removed on reuse or eviction. In-memory IP rate-limit windows last 60 seconds; inactive entries are removed under cache pressure or when the process ends.
  • Analytics choice: up to 180 days on your device. Google Analytics events follow our Google product retention settings and Google’s policy; contact us for the current configured event-retention window or a deletion request.
  • Provider logs and backups: subject to the hosting/database providers’ configured schedules. We do not claim instant deletion of isolated backups or use them to recreate deleted accounts. Contact us to establish the schedule applicable to your request.

At the end of a retention period we delete, anonymize or restrict the relevant records. A necessary legal hold is limited to data relevant to the obligation or claim.
